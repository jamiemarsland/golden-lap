# Golden Lap film: what only you can record

The first cut marks these with a dashed "TO SHOOT" box. Everything else is game footage or your own screenshots.

| Where (first cut time) | What to record | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 0:01 cold open | You at the webcam, hands up like a wheel, the game on screen behind you. Wide enough to see both hands. | It is the hook: nobody believes "I steer with my hands" until they see it. |
| 0:40 setup | The old onboard clip that started it, or you describing it to camera. | I have not used the original clip: it is someone else's footage. |
| 2:47 the idea | Your hands coming off the keyboard and going up into the wheel position. | The moment the film turns from keyboard to hands. |
| 2:55 hands | Screen recording of the game with the camera view visible, hands steering, ideally a whole lap with the helmet on. | The stills in the cut are small screenshots from our chat. A proper recording will look far better. |
| 4:07 payoff (optional) | Phone in hand as the wheel, with the car turning on the screen behind. | The cut only says it in words. |
